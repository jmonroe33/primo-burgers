# Primo Burgers website — Owner & staff guide

Everything on the website is changed from the **dashboard**. You never need to touch code.

> **The two save buttons**
> - Menu items, categories, Home page and About page have **Publish changes** — this puts your edit live. **Save draft** keeps it private until you're ready.
> - "Restaurant info & links" has a single **Save** button — it goes live right away.
>
> After publishing, the website updates within a few seconds. Refresh the page to see it.

---

## 1. Log in
1. Go to **yourwebsite.com/admin** (there's also a small *Staff login* link at the bottom of every page).
2. Enter your email and password → **Login**.
3. Forgot your password? Click **Forgot password?** on the login page, or ask the Owner to reset it.

After 5 wrong passwords the account is locked for 10 minutes.

The dashboard home page has big shortcut buttons for the most common jobs.

## 2. Change a menu price
1. Click **Edit menu & prices** (or **Menu → Menu items** in the left sidebar).
2. Type the dish in the search box (e.g. `bacon cheese`) and click its name.
3. Change **Price ($)** — e.g. `10.99`. Leave it blank if there's no fixed price, or use **Price note** (e.g. "Market price").
4. Click **Publish changes**. You'll see "Updated successfully".

**Changing many prices at once:** in the Menu items list, tick the boxes next to several items → **Edit** → choose the *Price* field.

**Prices not showing on the website?** Prices are hidden until you switch them on: **Restaurant info & links → Ordering → ☑ Show prices on the website → Save.** Untick it to hide all prices again (they stay saved).

## 3. Add a new menu item
1. **Menu → Menu items → Create New** (or the **Add a menu item** shortcut).
2. Fill in **Item name** and choose a **Category** (required). Add the price, a short description and confirmed ingredients if you have them.
3. Optional: upload a photo (see below), tick **Feature on home page**.
4. **Publish changes.**

To add a new **category** (e.g. "Desserts"): **Menu → Menu categories → Create New**, then **Publish changes**.
To change the **order** things appear in: open the Menu items or Menu categories list and drag the ⠿ handle on the left of a row.

## 4. Upload or replace pictures
1. Open the menu item → **Main photo** → **Create New** (upload from your computer/phone) or **Choose from existing**.
2. Fill in **Describe the photo** (e.g. "Bacon cheeseburger with fries") — this helps blind visitors and Google.
3. Save the photo, then **Publish changes** on the item.

Tips: landscape (sideways) photos look best; well-lit, close-up shots of the food sell best. Photos up to 10 MB; JPG, PNG, WebP or iPhone HEIC. They're automatically resized for fast loading.
To replace a photo, click the **×** on the current one and choose a new one. Items without photos show a simple icon with "Photo coming soon".

Home page banner and About page photos: **Website pages → Home page / About Us page**.

## 5. Mark an item unavailable (or hide it)
- **Sold out for now** — open the item → untick **Available right now** (right-hand side) → **Publish changes**. It stays on the menu with a "Temporarily unavailable" label. Tick it again when it's back.
- **Take it off the menu** — tick **Hide from website** → **Publish changes**. Nothing is deleted; untick to bring it back.
- **Delete forever** — open the item → ⋮ menu (top right) → **Delete** → confirm. Prefer *Hide* unless you're sure.

Hiding a whole **category** hides all of its items too.

## 6. Update business hours
1. **Restaurant info & links → Hours**.
2. Each row is one day. Type times like `7:00 AM` and `9:00 PM`, or tick **Closed**.
3. For holidays use **Hours note** (e.g. "Closed Thanksgiving Day").
4. **Save.** The "Open now / Closed" badge on the website updates automatically.

Phone and address are on the **Contact & location** tab of the same page.

## 7. Change ordering & social media links
**Restaurant info & links → Ordering**
- **DoorDash store link** / **Uber Eats store link** — open your store page in DoorDash/Uber Eats, copy the full address from the browser, paste it here. The website only accepts real doordash.com / ubereats.com links.
- Leave a link blank and its button disappears from the website.
- An individual dish can have its own DoorDash/Uber Eats link (open the item → *Item-specific delivery links*). Usually not needed.

**Restaurant info & links → Social media** — paste your full Instagram / Facebook / TikTok / YouTube page links. Blank = hidden.

**Announcement bar** tab — tick *Show announcement bar*, type a short message (e.g. "Now open Sundays!") → **Save**. Untick to remove it.

## 8. Publish changes — and undo mistakes
- **Publish changes** = live on the website. **Save draft** = saved, but customers still see the old version.
- **Undo:** open the item → **Versions** tab (top right) → pick an earlier version → **Restore**.

## 9. The menu QR code
**Restaurant info & links → QR code** — download **PNG** (for printing yourself), **SVG** (for a print shop), or open the printable 4×6" table card. The code always opens the online menu, so you **never need to reprint it** when prices or items change.
If you see a red "Don't print this yet" warning, the website isn't on its permanent address yet — ask your developer.

## 10. Staff accounts (Owner/Admin only)
**Settings → Staff accounts → Create New** — enter email, a password, and choose **Staff editor** (can edit menu & content) or **Owner / Admin** (can also manage accounts). To remove someone's access, open their account and delete it.
